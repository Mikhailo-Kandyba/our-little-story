Date-choice photos
==================
Place atmospheric photos here with these filenames:

  romantic-dinner.jpg  (or .png / .jpeg)
  theatre-date.jpg
  cabin-date.jpg
  surprise-date.jpg
  cinema-date.jpg
  mini-trip.jpg

Then update dateChoices[].image paths in src/js/siteConfig.js if the extension changes.

Current files are soft-color PNG placeholders until real photos are added.
